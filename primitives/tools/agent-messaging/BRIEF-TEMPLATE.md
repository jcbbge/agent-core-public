# Brief template (an NQ hand-off)

Every brief is an NQ hand-off under the [agent contract](../../rules/agent-contract/RULE.md). `agent-spawn`
refuses a brief missing any of the six sections below. Fill each in for the receiver's job; link details
rather than pasting transcripts.

# Purpose and outcome
Why this work exists, and the tangible result it must produce (file, code change or written response).

# Parties
Who the receiver is, who sent the work (name, session, pane, host), and what each owes the other.

# Provisioning
Verified sources, existing decisions, resources, access, tools and exact locations the receiver needs. Also
the numbered working list, whose last item is always: reap.

# Scope and authority
What is permitted, what is excluded, what must be left alone.

# Expected response and evidence
What the receiver returns, and the exact checks on the live system that prove it is done.

# Escalation
Any problem goes to the sender with `agent-send`, then the receiver waits for the answer. Three answered
NQ turns with questions left means the work holds and the human is alerted.

The receiver's first message is its NQ confirmation: its understanding, the numbered list, and answers to
What is unclear? What is ambiguous? Do I have everything I need to proceed?
