# Nonconvergence

The target is **fluent obviousness**: a familiar mechanism presented with the confidence and vocabulary of a discovery. Expose the default so a renamed version cannot return unnoticed. Seek a useful causal difference, not merely surprising prose.

## Expose the basin

Briefly name:

- **Default:** the answer a capable practitioner would ordinarily give.
- **Nearest alternatives:** the other immediate answers and whether they change the mechanism or merely its packaging.
- **Constraint to preserve:** what the default gets right that the replacement must still deliver.
- **Unresolved limitation:** the specific reason exploration is worth doing here.

Set the default aside during generation; do not declare it disproven. If there is no meaningful unresolved limitation, or the request has a known-good answer, deliver that answer. An evidence-supported return to the default is not a failure; disguising it as a new discovery is.

## Change the generating assumption

Restate the problem without its familiar domain nouns: what must be preserved, allocated, inferred, coordinated, or made observable, under which constraints?

Generate alternatives by changing a load-bearing premise rather than adding features. For example: does the decision need to happen at all; is the bottleneck information, authority, or timing; is the apparent resource scarce or merely difficult to transfer? These are candidate reframings, not conclusions.

Keep branches distinct by their causal move: **what changes → why that helps → what still limits it**. Merge synonyms. Keep genuinely incompatible explanations separate until evidence distinguishes them; do not manufacture a consensus or a hybrid that inherits both costs.

## Import mechanisms, not costumes

For a distant domain, supply:

1. A constraint it shares with this task.
2. A concrete mechanism practitioners use to handle that constraint.
3. A reliable source or artifact supporting that mechanism, actually supplied or inspected in this task. A recalled title or model-generated citation is not verification. Without accessible evidence, mark the transfer speculative; do not invent a study, statistic, or observed result to justify it.
4. The assumption under which the mechanism transfers—and a fact that would break it.

Work one domain to depth before recruiting another. For a substantial exploration, use a second domain to attack the first transfer, not merely decorate it with another analogy. Distance is useful only when the transfer survives. Do not reject a valid familiar field merely because it is familiar, or invoke quantum mechanics, evolution, or jazz as a substitute for a mechanism.

Try exporting the surviving mechanism to another setting. If it holds only by resemblance, narrow the claim. A useful local result need not be a universal principle.

## Separate generation from challenge

First develop the candidate; then test it against the preserved constraint and an adverse case. Prefer feedback from an artifact, experiment, or genuinely independent reviewer when available and proportionate. Multiple personas in one response are not independent observations, and this skill does not require spawning agents.

Use these interventions only when they change the work:

- **Amplify:** a branch produces a new testable consequence.
- **Inhibit:** it repeats the default, imports an unsupported assumption, or adds decorative complexity.
- **Rupture:** exploration changes vocabulary but not the causal model; replace a premise or obtain a discriminating observation.
- **Exit:** an answer is supported, no available test can distinguish the remaining branches, or further exploration no longer changes the decision.

Do not claim to inspect hidden activations, compute a conceptual hash, measure basin width, or certify distance from the training distribution. Compare mechanisms explicitly instead.

## Retain only what survives

A candidate must preserve the real constraints, explain a specific advantage over the baseline, survive a serious counterexample, and remain meaningful when stated plainly. Identify the cheapest observation that could make you abandon it. No evidence yet means a proposal, not a discovery.

Test for ornament: if the passage can be pasted unchanged onto unrelated tasks, remove or specialize it. If the long explanation contains no claim absent from the short version, compress it. Do not delete uncertainty or an inconvenient limitation to make the idea sound stronger.

Return the baseline limitation, the surviving alternative or live disagreement, its mechanism, its failure boundary, and the next discriminating test. Cite verified sources where used. Do not narrate a theatrical struggle against the distribution or declare something unprecedented without evidence.

**Keep the non-obvious mechanism. Discard the performance of being non-obvious.**
